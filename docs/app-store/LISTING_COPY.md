## Description (4000 chars max; not search-indexed — written to convert, not to rank)

```
Your brain is for having thoughts, not holding them.

Offload is the fastest way to get something out of your head and trust it
comes back exactly when it's useful. Speak a thought — "grab milk at
Safeway," "ask about the brake noise at the shop," "measure the hallway
when I'm home" — and Offload does the rest. It turns your words into a
clean note, figures out which real-world place it belongs to, and quietly
waits. The moment you arrive, your note is there.

No lists to maintain. No app to remember to open. No overdue-task guilt.
You offload the thought once, and the place remembers it for you.

HOW IT WORKS

1. Speak it. Tap record and say what's on your mind. Live transcription
   shows your words as you talk.
2. Offload files it. Your note is transcribed, cleaned up, and matched to
   the place you mentioned — the Safeway near you, not one across the
   country.
3. It comes back on arrival. When you get there, the reminder fires —
   right on your phone, even without a signal.

WHAT MAKES OFFLOAD DIFFERENT

• Places remember, so you don't have to. Notes accumulate on the places in
  your life. Walk into the hardware store and everything you've ever meant
  to get there is waiting.
• Built for leaky working memory. If reminder apps have never stuck for
  you — if lists rot and to-dos become guilt — Offload asks nothing of you
  after the moment of capture.
• Arrival detection runs on your device. Reminders fire from your phone's
  own region monitoring, so they work in dead zones and never depend on a
  server knowing where you are.

PRIVACY, PLAINLY

• Your voice is never stored. Audio is transcribed and immediately
  discarded — only the text survives.
• Your location is never tracked. Arrival checks happen on your device;
  your coordinates aren't uploaded when you cross into a saved place, and
  no movement history exists anywhere.
• Your notes are yours. Nothing is used to train AI models, and nothing is
  sold or shared with advertisers. Ever.
• Delete everything, any time, from Settings — immediate and permanent.

Full policy: https://www.useoffload.app/privacy

Offload is for the busy parent running errands, the tradesperson juggling
job sites, the person whose best ideas arrive in the car — anyone whose
thoughts outrun their memory.

Get it out of your head. Offload keeps it.

Questions or ideas? support@useoffload.app
```
